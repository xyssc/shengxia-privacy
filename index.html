Privacy Notice

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Notice describes how Shengxia Changqing (Guangzhou) Trading Co., Ltd. ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with our internal use of the Amazon Ads API.

1. Purpose of Amazon Ads API Access

We use the Amazon Ads API solely to access, analyze, manage, and optimize advertising campaigns associated with our own Amazon advertising accounts.

Our use of the API may include retrieving advertising reports, analyzing campaign and search-term performance, monitoring advertising metrics, and managing our own advertising campaigns.

We do not provide Amazon Ads API access or advertising management services to third-party advertisers through this application.

2. Information We Process

In connection with the Amazon Ads API, we may process advertising and campaign information made available through authorized Amazon advertising accounts, such as campaign information, advertising performance metrics, search-term reporting data, and related advertising data.

3. How We Use Information

Information obtained through the Amazon Ads API is used for internal business purposes, including advertising performance analysis, campaign optimization, reporting, and advertising management.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell information obtained through the Amazon Ads API. Access to advertising information is limited to authorized personnel and service providers where necessary for legitimate business and technical operations.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for our advertising management, reporting, legal, and business purposes, subject to applicable requirements.

7. Amazon Services

Our use of Amazon advertising services and the Amazon Ads API is also subject to applicable Amazon agreements, policies, and requirements.

8. Changes to This Privacy Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice when our practices or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
